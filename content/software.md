**drcmd**: Doubly-robust causal inference with missing data (R package). Available on [GitHub](https://github.com/keithbarnatchez/drcmd) | [Documentation](/drcmd/) | [Launch app](https://keithbarnatchez.shinyapps.io/drcmd-app/)

**survstrat**: Estimation of longitudinal survivor causal effects, as well as representativeness measures of the underlying survivor stratum (R package). Available on [GitHub](https://github.com/keithbarnatchez/survstrat).
